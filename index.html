What We Do

StuySkates brings together ice skating enthusiasts of all skill levels across Stuyvesant. Our club offers a supportive community, trips to local rinks, and bonding events throughout the year. From total beginners to experienced skaters, everyone is welcome. Beyond the ice, we hope to run club meetings and social events to help you meet fellow skaters.
